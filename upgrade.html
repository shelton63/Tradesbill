Common questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — cancel anytime from Settings → Plan. No long-term contracts, no exit fees. Your account stays active until the end of the billing period.
What happens when my free trial ends?
You'll still be able to log in and see your data, but you won't be able to send new invoices until you upgrade. No data is ever deleted.
Do you take a cut of my payments?
No — Primo Invoice doesn't take any cut. Stripe's standard processing fee applies (typically 1.5% for UK cards), but that goes to Stripe, not us.
Can I change plans later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you only pay the difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
Is my payment data secure?
All payments are processed by Stripe — the same technology used by Amazon, Apple, and millions of other businesses. We never store your card details.
